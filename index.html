  • MILK CHOCOLATE

    Milk chocolate is a solid chocolate confectionery containing cocoa, sugar and milk. It is the most consumed type of chocolate, originally sold solely as a chocolate bar.

  • MILK

    Milk is a white liquid food produced by the mammary glands of mammals. It is the primary source of nutrition for young mammals before they are able to digest solid food.

  • NUTS

    Nuts are nutrient-dense seeds with a hard shell, widely used in confectionery. Roasted hazelnuts add a rich, crunchy texture and a warm, earthy flavor to chocolate.

  • SEMI-SWEET CHOCOLATE

    Semi-sweet chocolate has a rich, deep cocoa flavor with just a hint of sweetness. Containing 35% to 65% cocoa solids, it balances bitterness and sugar for baking and snacking.

  • CACAO

    Cocoa is a natural, unsweetened powder made from roasted and ground cacao beans. It gives chocolate its signature deep color, rich aroma, and slightly bitter taste.

  • RASPBERRY

    Raspberry is a sweet-tart berry packed with vibrant flavor and antioxidants. Paired with rich chocolate, it creates a refreshing, fruity contrast in every bite.

  • COCONUT OIL

    Coconut oil is a natural plant fat extracted from mature coconuts. It gives chocolate a smooth, melt-in-your-mouth texture and a subtle tropical aroma.

How it's made?

Try our chocolates today and discover the perfect balance of flavor

  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan without bringing it to a boil. Add sugar and cocoa powder.

  2. At the same time, prepare a water bath. Melt the butter on it.

  3. "Mix" the milk mixture and already liquid butter. Mix thoroughly with a silicone spatula so that the mass becomes uniform and silky.

  4. Heat the mixture over low heat, letting it barely boil.

  5. Pour the chocolate into special containers (can be replaced with ice molds). Allow to cool and harden.
